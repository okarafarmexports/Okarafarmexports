Simple, transparent ordering.

  1. Send your enquiryTell us the vegetables, quantity and delivery port you need.
  2. Receive our quotationWe reply with clear per-ton pricing in USD and a product specification sheet.
  3. Confirm the orderWe issue a proforma invoice. Payment is by bank transfer: 40% advance to begin procurement and 60% balance against shipping documents.
  4. Sorting and packingVegetables are collected, sorted and packed at our warehouse in Okara.
  5. Cold-chain dispatchThe container is loaded, sealed and shipped, and scanned documents are sent to you before discharge.

Our trial shipment is one 40ft reefer container (28 metric tons), with weekly repeat containers available.
